Privacy Policy for Plantify

Published by Allcore Solution • Last Updated: November 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Plantify, owned and operated by Allcore Solution ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data when you use the Plantify mobile application and related botanical nursery delivery services.

By downloading, accessing, or using Plantify, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

To provide you with plant ordering, nursery fulfillment, and live tracking services, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Personal Identification: Name, email address, contact phone number when you create an account, sign in with Google, or place an order.
  • Delivery Addresses: Street address, flat/house number, city, state, postal PIN code to safely deliver nursery plants and botanical supplies.
  • Location Data (Precise/Coarse GPS): When using live delivery route tracking or delivery address selection, we access location data with your explicit permission to verify delivery availability and display courier transit route progress.
  • Order & Purchase Information: Plant items purchased, total amount, order status, delivery notes, and transaction identifiers.
  • Device & Technical Diagnostics: Device model, OS version, Firebase app analytics, and performance crash logs to maintain app stability.
💳 Payment Security Note: We do not store your credit card numbers, debit card PINs, CVV codes, or net banking passwords. All payments are processed through secure, RBI-authorized, encrypted payment gateways.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for legitimate business purposes including:

  • Processing, packing, and dispatching your botanical plant orders from our nursery hubs.
  • Providing real-time order tracking and dispatch status updates via push notifications.
  • Customer care support, plant replacement requests, and order inquiries.
  • Authenticating user sessions and safeguarding against unauthorized access or fraud.
  • Internal nursery operations and store inventory synchronization.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted third-party services that process data in compliance with strict privacy standards:

  • Google Firebase: Used for secure cloud authentication, Firestore database synchronization, and push notifications.
  • Google Maps Platform: Used for address geocoding and delivery route display.
  • Payment Gateways (UPI, Razorpay / Cashfree): Secure transaction authorization.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion Policy

In accordance with Google Play Store Developer Policies, you have the right to request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time.

How to delete your account:
• In-App: Open Plantify > Go to Account > Tap Delete My Account > Confirm deletion.
• Via Email: Email our privacy team at contact@allcoresolution.com with the subject line "Request Data Deletion - Plantify" from your registered email. Your account and personal identifiable information will be permanently purged within 30 business days.

6. Security of Your Information

We implement industry-standard physical, technical, and administrative security measures (including HTTPS/TLS encrypted data transmission) to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

7. Children's Privacy

Our app and services are not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.
